Life.hack keyboard controls:

0-9          Load prestored board (0 = logo board)
Space        Compute Next Generation
Enter        Free-run until another key pressed
Arrows/WASD  Move editing cursor up/down/right/left
E            Toggle current cell
DEL          Clear board
< or ,       Save board in temp buffer (2 levels)
> or .       Restore board from temp buffer (2 levels)
